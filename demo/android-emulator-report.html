Tested Feature: YouTube Video Sharing

Proves that the application can be shared a YouTube video via authentic Android native intents:

1. Browser Link Share (ACTION_VIEW)

Receives standard browser URL (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ), updates player and loads subtitles.

2. YouTube App Text Share (ACTION_SEND)

Receives share text from official YouTube app ("Check out this video: https://youtu.be/kJQP7kiw5Fk"), extracts video ID and switches active stream.
